SETTINGS

Illustrations drawn from the suite's own icons, type and tokens, not screenshots. Hover or tab to any skin. Scroll over Volume or SignalRGB; click the Volume percentage, the Wi-Fi icon, Uptime, the Fan Control snowflake, EMPTY or the DeepCool logo.

THE SKINS

Every skin follows the same rules: a quiet icon or number at rest, more on hover, a right-click menu that opens its page in Settings. Each has a guide that lists its options and what it needs.

  • DAY-DATE

    The day name over the date. Click opens the Windows calendar.

  • POWER

    Hibernate (or sleep), shut down, restart and lock. A schedule link per icon opens a scroll picker from 15 minutes to 24 hours, with a countdown. Shut down and restart ask twice.

  • UPTIME

    Invisible until hovered. Turns orange and says RESTART RECOMMENDED after five days (you choose how many).

  • VOLUME

    A ring at the current level. Hover for the percentage and the output device. Scroll to change, click the ring to jump to a level, click the percentage to mute, click the device for the next output.

  • MEDIA

    The song playing in any app. The ring is progress and play/pause. Title, artist and previous and next arrows appear on hover only. Hides itself when nothing plays.

  • RECYCLE BIN

    The trash icon, the total size on hover (orange when large) and an EMPTY link when there is something to empty.

  • PLAYNITE

    The controller icon that opens Playnite, skipping its splash screen. Hides itself if Playnite is not installed.

  • WI-FI

    Signal strength as lit arcs. Hover for the network name and a speed test. Warnings show at rest: NO INTERNET, NOT CONNECTED, AIRPLANE MODE, ETHERNET.

  • FAN CONTROL

    A fan icon, the average speed on hover and a snowflake that sends every fan to 100% and back, through the Fan Control app.

  • SIGNALRGB

    Case light brightness by scrolling, plus a double arrow that starts a move effect. Works with the free tier and uses the local API on Pro.

  • DEEPCOOL

    A switch for the DeepCool app that drives the case screen. Accent while it runs; STARTING and STOPPING TELEMETRY show until the process confirms.

  • CONTROLLERS

    One row per connected controller (Xbox, DualSense, Moza wheel). A battery shows at rest only when low; hover shows all of them. Low is orange, charging is accent.

  • SETTINGS BUTTON

    A dim gear that opens the Settings window. Never hides itself, so there is always a way back in.

DESIGN

Skins share a design language, not a layout. The rules are few and they are applied everywhere.

  • QUIET AT REST

    Icons sit at 60% brightness. Anything you do not need to read all the time waits for the pointer.

  • KEY INFORMATION STAYS

    Volume level, bin size, warnings and low batteries are visible without hovering. Or choose ALWAYS and see everything.

  • SUBTLE HOVER

    A skin rises to 98% and the ring or icon takes the accent. No underlines, boxes or glow.

  • SHARP TYPE AND RINGS

    Segoe UI Variable in spaced capitals; rings drawn as true arcs with round caps.

  • YOUR ACCENT

    Follows the Windows accent colour, or a custom one. On light wallpapers it darkens so it stays readable.

SETTINGS

Right-click any skin and choose Control-Plaine settings. One window, a tab per skin, changes apply at once. No file editing.

  • LEFT OR RIGHT

    Every skin except Power can sit on either screen edge and keeps the same footprint. Set one or all at once.

  • LIGHT AND DARK

    Colours for dark wallpapers, light wallpapers, or follow Windows. Any skin can differ.

  • DETAILS AND TOOLTIPS

    Details on hover or always, tooltips on or off, per skin or for all.

  • SIZE AND BRIGHTNESS

    Scale every skin from 50% to 300%, and tune the rest and hover brightness.

  • FULL SCREEN AND LOCK

    Hide every skin while a game or video is full screen. Lock positions against stray drags.

  • WHAT IT NEEDS

    A skin whose app or plugin is missing gets an amber ! and says what to install instead of sitting broken.

INSTALL

Needs Rainmeter 4.5 or newer on Windows 10 or 11. From the repo folder in PowerShell:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\install.ps1
  1. 1

    INSTALL

    Links the repo into your Skins folder, creates your settings files, installs the plugins the skins need and loads the Control-Plaine layout. Your current desktop is saved as a backup layout first.

  2. 2

    SET UP

    Settings opens a short guided setup: which skins you want, what each needs (with the exact install command, run only when you click), the look, and where the skins sit on your screen.

  3. 3

    USE

    Hover, scroll and click. Right-click any skin to change anything. Update later with a git pull and a refresh.
